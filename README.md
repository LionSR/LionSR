# Hi, I’m Sirui Lu 👋

I work at the intersection of **quantum many-body physics and AI**: physics for AI, and AI for physics. I am a physics PhD candidate at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics and TU Munich, advised by J. Ignacio Cirac.

My research spans quantum algorithms and simulation, the physics of generative models, and AI agents that develop and verify mathematical arguments.

## 🔬 Research and software

- 🤖 **[TeXRA](https://texra.ai)** — an agent harness for theoretical research, connecting language models to symbolic computation and Lean proofs.
- ✓ **[FormalFlow / MIPStarRE](https://github.com/LionSR/MIPStarRE)** — long-horizon autoformalization of the quantum soundness of the classical low individual-degree test, a core theorem underlying MIP\* = RE. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19814)
- 🕸️ **[TNLean](https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean)** — tensor-network theory in Lean 4 and Mathlib, including the fundamental theorem of matrix-product states. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07857)
- ⚛️ **[QICLean](https://github.com/LionSR/QICLean)** — formalized quantum information theory.
- 🛡️ **[Lean-verified quantum codes](https://github.com/LionSR/lean-qec-sslp)** — agent-assisted search with exact verification, including a catalogue of 14,116 certified quantum error-correcting codes. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20728)
- 📦 **[Agentic Publication Protocol](https://github.com/LionSR/AgenticPublicationProtocol)** — publishing research with the code, data, and instructions that agents need to reproduce and extend it.
